# Prior work and scope

Reviewed 21 November 2026. The claim is an exact identity for an extremum of this split–inert prime-ideal race with variable window width, its single-prime-power specialization and the two-term cusp with a moving width. The underlying correspondence between zeros and prime powers is classical.

| Predecessor | Relation to the result |
|---|---|
| Guinand–Weil and Poisson–Newton formulas | Explicit formulas transport spectral information to local prime-power masses. Muñoz and Pérez-Marco explain the general trace-formula setting. Here positivity identifies a particular extremum of the prime-only score, and a dilation difference leaves only the requested finite band. [Muñoz and Pérez-Marco](https://arxiv.org/abs/1309.1449) |
| Landau and Gonek | Landau's sums over zeta zeros distinguish prime powers. Uniform variants account for the distance to a nearby prime power. Our parameter is the smoothing width in a three-height extremum, and the rescaled transition has an explicit triangular shape. [Baluyot and Gonek, introduction](https://msp.org/pjm/2019/303-1/pjm-v303-n1-p02-s.pdf) |
| Second differences and triangular prime sums | Dudek, Grenié and Molteni difference an integrated Chebyshev sum to localize its arithmetic side. Exponential splines and exact discrete operators have also been studied systematically. The compact-support cancellation here belongs to those established methods. [Dudek, Grenié and Molteni](https://sites.unimi.it/molteni/research/papers-pdf/36-molteni-Primes_in_explicit_short_intervals_on_RH.pdf) · [Unser and Blu](https://bigwww.epfl.ch/publications/unser0503.html) |
| Variable-width prime sums | The ordinary-prime extremal formula already evaluates the peak by a compact sum over prime powers below the width. The earlier result for this field gives oscillation uniformly over fixed choices of widths and moves. The present observable isolates a band around the width and distinguishes its two limiting cusp coefficients. [Ordinary-prime extremum](https://github.com/leecrossley/prime-band-extrema/blob/d79c2c004855c423a240d82f8821232c1da849b6/README.md) · [547/727 width family](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-race-short-shifts/blob/483c31f2df2132ed2165620ea55e20e969228bfb/PROOF.md) |
| Fixed-width curvature reconstruction | At width two, six or nine values near the specified log dilations recover a local Euler factor. Here the width is tuned to each prime power. The weighted contrast eliminates all powers outside its band, including the dyadic neighbours of powers of two. The ordinary contrast has a separate moving-width asymptotic. [Fixed-width reconstruction](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-race-curvature-euler-factors/blob/af7093b56a7f6b4155dba27e735b5b1d471ff707/PROOF.md) |
| Primitive strip theorem | The positive real-frequency series assumes GRH, which Alpöge's announced, unrefereed Dirichlet GRH theorem supplies. The public Lean development and the records of independent kernel and statement comparisons are pinned in the replay. [Announcement](https://alpo.ge/remark.pdf) [Upstream theorem](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/371a1a26d5e17f604428e3a8ce6ce341cf517434/src/Main.lean#L12) [Sutherland's checks](https://math.mit.edu/~drew/grh/) |
