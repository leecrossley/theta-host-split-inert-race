# Prior work and scope

The results use standard mean-square orthogonality and finite-dimensional prediction. The arithmetic question is how much of a fixed theta prime-ideal race a different field can recover from all compact signed filters, even after adding a prescribed number of the target field's own past values. The certificates estimate the error for both directions of prediction and for logarithmic observation on fixed powers.

| Source | Relation to this result |
|---|---|
| Akbary, Ng and Shahabi | Their almost-periodic framework treats distributions and mean squares of arithmetic error terms, including Chebotarev counts. Parseval's identity supplies the diagonal energy in the bounded case here. [Limiting distributions of the classical error terms of prime number theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.1657) |
| de la Bretèche, Fiorilli and Jouve | Their work studies moments for smoothed Chebotarev counts using positive expressions and zeros of Artin factors. It provides a broader setting for retaining a subset of the zero contributions. [Moments in the Chebotarev density theorem](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.12899) |
| Banks, and Banks and Loftus | Banks compares the zero sets of distinct primitive characters of the same modulus. Banks and Loftus study nonvanishing of combinations of Dirichlet functions at zeros of a given factor. This computation requires a critical-line zero of the target and simultaneous nonvanishing of all eight members of the opposite product at that point. It checks 710 specific brackets and makes no extrapolation to all high zeros. [Comparing zeros](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.07073) · [Unshared zeros](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22930) |
| Linear prediction | Minimizing Toeplitz quadratic energy with a monic predictor yields the usual prediction-error variance. The second numerical proof uses the classical reflection-coefficient recursion and a rigorous correction for uncertain arithmetic moments. [Durbin, The fitting of time-series models](https://doi.org/10.2307/1401322) |
| Earlier arithmetic work | The original trace papers establish the two fields. The joint-levels certificate isolates nine low frequencies and their mixed sums. The compact-filter paper proves pointwise two-sided bounds for one host's delay vectors. Its full-strip formula and fixed-power sampling estimate are used here. This result adds the 710 two-product exclusions, projection against every compact comparison filter, a mean-square growth dichotomy and 32 uniform RMS bounds with both target and comparison data. [Trace hosts](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-host-split-inert-race/releases/tag/v1.0.0) · [Joint levels and moves](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-host-split-inert-race/releases/tag/joint-levels-and-moves-v1.0.0) · [Compact-filter theorem](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-host-split-inert-race/releases/tag/compact-filter-oscillation-v1.0.0) |

Searches on 21 November 2026 covered both conductor pairs, unshared Dirichlet zeros, Chebotarev mean square and covariance, cross-field prime-race prediction, logarithmic fixed-power sampling and compact linear filters. The sources examined yielded no earlier statement or numerical certificate for the two fields, the forecast class and these RMS bounds. This is a scoped priority check. The mean-square, prediction and distinct-zero methods have the published precedents above.
