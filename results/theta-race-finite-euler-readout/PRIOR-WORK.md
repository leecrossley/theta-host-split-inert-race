# Prior work and scope

Reviewed 21 November 2026. The claim concerns exact finite-window measurement of the primitive Euler denominator on this 547/727 prime-ideal race. The arithmetic of the local Euler factors, second differences of piecewise affine functions and finite geometric-series inversion are established methods.

| Source | Relation to this result |
|---|---|
| Dudek, Grenié and Molteni | Their symmetric difference of the integrated Chebyshev function selects a triangularly weighted sum of prime powers. Here the input is already a smoothed prime-ideal count, and a second difference at a quarter-integer grid isolates its three integer-norm knots. [Section 2 of the arithmetic progression paper](https://air.unimi.it/retrieve/dfa8b99e-9aeb-748b-e053-3a05fe0a3a96/43-molteni-Explicit_short_intervals_for_primes_in_arithmetic_progressions_on_GRH.pdf#page=4) |
| Vetterli, Marziliano and Blu | They reconstruct streams of point masses and splines from samples. The three known knots of each mass here allow a direct scalar difference and a finite inverse along each dyadic chain. [Sampling Signals With Finite Rate of Innovation](https://www-inst.cs.berkeley.edu/~ee123/sp17/resources/vetterli0201.pdf) |
| Finite ordinary-prime ramp sums | Earlier work for this weight uses its piecewise affine dependence on the starting height to bound a sum throughout each cell. The new use is to extract the exact local mass and decode the eight-character factor. [Finite ramp calculations](https://github.com/leecrossley/three-halves-prime-window-1e28/blob/63f6a426faa3f8de794a4cbeb3d61c2fc0a1d100/README.md) · [Prime-window weight](https://github.com/leecrossley/prime-band-critical-all-scales/blob/ecb4538f96452cd25fff0d10fab52f71117d57ea/README.md) |
| The concrete theta frame | The field construction, character group, split and inert signs, and residue-degree formula were established for this field. They provide the arithmetic identification used in the inverse. [Field proof](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-race-short-shifts/blob/483c31f2df2132ed2165620ea55e20e969228bfb/PROOF.md) |
| Reconstruction from extremal curvature | Six fixed-width limiting values, or two appropriately tuned width measurements with known degree, recover even the Euler factors at omitted primes. Those limiting results assume GRH. The present good-prime decoder is unconditional, uses three actual finite scores, needs only norms through the prime, and has an explicit sharp scalar rounding threshold. Its omitted factors come from conductor data. [Fixed-width extrema](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-race-curvature-euler-factors/blob/af7093b56a7f6b4155dba27e735b5b1d471ff707/PROOF.md) · [Tuned-width readout](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-race-width-cusp/blob/52eee965886958193d2ff38595f92389b90f4b93/PROOF.md) |
