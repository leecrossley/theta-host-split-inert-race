# Prior work and scope

The field-specific result is a two-sided deadline on a prescribed geometric observation grid, uniformly in its offset and a continuum of sampling ratios. At ratio two we obtain 223 and 319 consecutive starts. At every ratio from two through four we obtain 4,095. A subgrid argument gives a positive lower uniform index density throughout $`1\lt r\le4`$. The proof also exhibits the obstruction to extending a single finite block length to unbounded ratios.

The Fejér polynomial, the triangular average, the chord bound, and uniform almost periodicity are classical. Searches on 21 November 2026 covered quantitative prime-race oscillations on dyadic and geometric progressions, sampling compact prime-ideal sums, the four conductor labels, and sign changes with bounded gaps at powers of two. The sources examined contained no earlier instance of these two field-specific bounds, their spacing covers or the dyadic-density constants. Unindexed and unpublished work is outside this comparison.

Every statement invokes Alpöge's announced primitive Dirichlet critical-line theorem for the odd factors and the even base factors; this is the Dirichlet GRH input. The genuine zero brackets and their 28,000 endpoint signs are finite computations. The public proof records which analytic and numerical steps each check covers.
