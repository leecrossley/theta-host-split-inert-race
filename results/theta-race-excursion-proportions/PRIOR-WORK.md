# Prior work and scope

Reviewed 21 November 2026. I found no earlier result giving these finite-interval percentages or the 15% lower uniform logarithmic density for each sign of the dyadic increment on the 547/727 field. The statement fixes the score, the field, the threshold $`1.9735\log2`$ and the measure on starting heights.

| Source | Relation to this result |
|---|---|
| Akbary, Ng and Shahabi | Their work proves general limiting-distribution theorems for arithmetic error terms and includes Chebotarev errors. The present claim gives numerical lower bounds on every translated finite interval for a particular local change. [Limiting distributions of the classical error terms](https://www.cs.uleth.ca/~nathanng/RESEARCH/AkbaryNgShahabi.pdf) |
| Martin and Ng | Their inclusivity results weaken the independence assumptions for races of primes in residue classes. Here an explicit separation check for finitely many sums and differences suffices for four moments. [Inclusive prime number races](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.00088) |
| Leung | This paper studies joint distributions of prime counts in short intervals under hypotheses on zeta zeros. The observable, field, finite guarantees and method of controlling the frequency relations differ. [Joint distribution of primes in multiple short intervals](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.04000) |
| Bailleul and Hayani | They study limiting biases of orderings in prime-ideal races across conjugacy classes. The statistic here is a change in a weighted score when its starting scale doubles. [Prime ideal races with several competitors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.04087) |
| Earlier 547/727 results | The factor-400 result supplies the brackets and the absolute tail. The filtered deadline establishes the prime-only error and two individual moves near every height. The grid theorem finds small intervals of qualifying starts. The paired-window density concerns a different joint event with a fixed separation. [Bracket certificate](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-host-split-inert-race/blob/main/results/theta-race-factor-400/PROOF.md) · [Filtered deadline](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-host-split-inert-race/blob/main/results/theta-race-filtered-deadline/PROOF.md) · [Grid result](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-host-split-inert-race/blob/main/results/theta-race-log-grid/PROOF.md) · [Paired windows](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-host-split-inert-race/blob/main/results/theta-race-paired-density/PROOF.md) |
| Critical-line theorem | The analytic input is GRH for the primitive Dirichlet functions, including the conductor-one case for the base field. Alpöge's announced, unrefereed Dirichlet GRH theorem supplies it. [Pinned statement](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/371a1a26d5e17f604428e3a8ce6ce341cf517434/src/Main.lean#L12) [Independent kernel records](https://math.mit.edu/~drew/grh/) |

The search used the field pair, its radicand, weighted prime-ideal races, finite and uniform logarithmic proportions, short-interval prime distributions and fourth moments without linear independence. A public code search for both primes, “fourth moment” and “prime race” returned no matches. The new certificate uses classical moment inequalities and finite Fourier averaging. Its exact constants and finite arithmetic are available for replay. Unindexed or unpublished work could affect priority.
