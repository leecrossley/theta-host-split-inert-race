# Prior work and scope

Checked on 21 November 2026. Version 1.1.0 adds the finite guarantees $`400H`$ through $`e^{2000}`$ and $`256H`$ through $`e^{1000}`$, and the all-height endpoint $`e^{219}H^{17001/16999}`$ for changes greater than $`3.32\log2`$. The original claim under review was the explicit endpoint $`e^{162000}H^{3001/2999}`$, valid after every starting height for **both** signed dyadic changes of magnitude greater than $`1.9735\log 2`$ in the 547/727 split–inert prime-ideal score. The direct $`18H`$ certificate through one million concerns the same observable. No earlier statement or proof of this quantitative bound surfaced in the sources reviewed. Unpublished results and gaps in indexing limit any claim of priority.

## Finite heights and smaller power deadlines

The maintained extension adds two finite all-start guarantees, a positive Fejér test with a Logan window at every height, and a qualitative result for every fixed exponent greater than one. The earlier direct prime sieve reached $`10^9`$ with factor 18. The factor-400 obstruction concerns much larger heights and assumes Dirichlet GRH, which Alpöge's announced theorem supplies. The new finite proof reaches $`e^{1000}`$ with factor 256 and $`e^{2000}`$ with factor 400 using only finite zero verification. It estimates the positive ideal squares in physical prime coordinates, then verifies 1,543 signed averages with all unlisted frequencies bounded. [Direct sieve](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-race-factor-18/releases/tag/v1.0.1) · [Earlier Fourier-filter deadline](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-race-filtered-deadline/releases/tag/v1.0.0) · [Four-digit obstruction and assumptions](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-host-split-inert-race/releases/tag/theta-race-factor-400-v1.1.0)

It lowers the exponential prefactor from exp(162000) in the original result to exp(219), together with the smaller exponent. The Fejér polynomial and its positivity are classical.
