## Fixed-factor existence and the spectral alternative

Montgomery’s zero-density theorem implies a convergent reciprocal-ordinate sum for the zeros in every fixed half-strip strictly to the right of the critical line. The proof tilts the *observed move* at one such zero when present. After the tilt, the zeros needing exact cancellation belong to that sparse half-strip. Maximality of the selected real part at its own ordinate prevents an uncancellable positive real moment. The resulting bounded-width positive convolution handles a supremum which may never be attained. The complementary case uses the certified critical-line carrier. The theorem proves existence of a constant after every starting height. Its explicit polylogarithmic companion remains the numerical guarantee independent of unknown zero positions. [Montgomery, 1969](https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/mat564) · [Inoue, Lemma 1](https://www.numdam.org/item/10.5802/jtnb.1162.pdf)

Kaczorowski and Wiertelak count oscillations of prescribed size in initial ranges of arithmetic error terms. Morrill, Platt and Trudgian and the later work of Grześkowiak, Kaczorowski, Pańkowski and Radziejewski sharpen lower bounds for the number of sign changes of the ordinary Chebyshev error up to a height. An initial-range count alone allows gaps of unbounded log length. Kaczorowski and Pintz additionally localize oscillations in every sufficiently late power interval. The earlier sparse-observation result in this family identifies a limsup growth exponent. The present fixed-window conclusion locates both powers after every sufficiently late $`H`$ when any odd zero is off the line. [Prescribed oscillations](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9947-09-04803-X) · [Morrill, Platt and Trudgian](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.14203) · [Grześkowiak and coauthors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.10399) · [Earlier sparse-grid result](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-host-split-inert-race/releases/tag/compact-filter-oscillation-v1.0.0)
