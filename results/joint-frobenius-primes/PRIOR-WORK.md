# Prior work

Cullinan and Hajir determined thresholds for primes of prescribed congruence class in multiplicative intervals for small moduli. They combined explicit progression estimates with a finite computation. Bennett, Martin, O’Bryant and Rechnitzer gave explicit estimates for many small moduli. Here the modulus is 2,176,229,286,416 and the classes are grouped by four quadratic signs. The local trace map also identifies several sign patterns. [Cullinan and Hajir](https://faculty.bard.edu/cullinan/papers/prescribed_submitted.pdf) · [Bennett and collaborators](https://personal.math.ubc.ca/~bennett/BeMaObRe-IJM-2018.pdf)

Lee’s explicit prime number theorem under Dirichlet GRH supplies the bound used beyond the certified primes. The previous version relied on three entries from Bordignon’s Table 6. Formula (33) does not give those entries at the parameters used here. The separate computation of 63 real character bounds uses the Gauss sum for a primitive character to bound its partial sums. [Ethan S. Lee, Corollary 1.1 and Table 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2301.13457#page=2) [Numerical discrepancy](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-host-split-inert-race/issues/125) [Character-sum estimates](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.05114)

The use of certified prime chains and Proth congruences to cover a long finite range is classical. The present chains carry the four local character signs, bridge specified gaps to the infinite-range estimate and certify the two sharp threshold values. [Deshouillers and collaborators](https://math.dartmouth.edu/~carlp/PDF/paper91.pdf) · [Prime certificates](evidence/prime-chains.tsv)

The existing theta-host work establishes the local trace decomposition on both fields. The algebraic-height result obtains two nonparallel local directions by bounding first and second moments. That moment argument leaves open whether a specified corner, trace pair or four-sign pattern occurs in a given interval. The full-modulus estimate and the labelled prime chains answer those local questions here. The interval variable in this result is an ordinary prime cutoff. [Local decomposition](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-host-split-inert-race/blob/joint-levels-and-moves-v1.2.0/results/joint-levels-and-moves/PROOF.md#reproducing-the-numerical-certificate) · [Earlier projected prime bound](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-host-split-inert-race/blob/algebraic-heights-v1.0.0/results/algebraic-heights/PROOF.md#an-explicit-infinite-range-and-a-prime-bound)

The search covered both conductor pairs together, the two endpoint fractions and prime gaps, prescribed quadratic signatures, multiplicative prime intervals, effective Chebotarev estimates, prime ladders and the maintained family index. I found no earlier publication of these two sharp real-start thresholds, the complete failure sets at factors at least $`4/3`$ for these fields or these explicit sixteen-pattern mass bounds.
