# Prior work

The 547/727 and 571/599 traces, odd Dirichlet products and the signed prime-square correction were established in the host and joint-race papers. The first-failure certificate located the first missed $`400H`$ move and showed that a failure of the localization forces an odd zero below $`e^{810330}`$. Its unrestricted tail uses the inverse-square decay of the pointwise tent transform. The present result gives a finite reconstruction of each unsmoothed move on either field and lowers that ordinate endpoint to $`e^{405161}`$. [Two host races](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-host-split-inert-race/releases/tag/joint-levels-and-moves-v1.2.0) · [First-failure certificate](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-host-split-inert-race/releases/tag/theta-race-factor-400-v1.2.0)

Compact smoothing, explicit formulas, Fourier damping and recovery of prime sums from verified zeros have substantial prior literature. Büthe obtains explicit classical prime-counting bounds from finite RH verification, and Johnston extends the range of several such estimates. Their test functions and target error bounds differ from the pointwise normalized differences in the degree-eight hosts here. Montgomery and Vaughan supply the short-interval prime estimate and McCurley the unconditional base-field zero control. [Büthe](https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.7015) · [Johnston](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.02249) · [Montgomery and Vaughan](https://doi.org/10.1112/S0025579300004708) · [McCurley](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0022314X84900891)

The specific argument exploits exact cancellation on the exponential pieces of the prime tent. Only nearby prime-norm breakpoints contribute to the recovery error. The variance of a box convolution and an unconditional short-interval prime count give the cutoff $`O(y^{1/4}\sqrt{\log y})`$ for fixed absolute accuracy. The statement concerns the actual complex zeros and the actual unsmoothed prime-only move.

I searched the two field pairs, weighted prime sums with fourth-root cutoffs, finite Dirichlet zero verification, recovery from smoothed explicit formulas and the existing family index on 21 November 2026. I found no earlier source for this explicit two-field reconstruction at tolerance 0.0008 or for the two reduced exceptional regions. The general smoothing and zero-counting methods are classical.

## Pure fourth-root and direct truncations

The box formula in the first release had cutoff $`8\cdot10^6e^{x/4}\sqrt{\lceil x\rceil+1}`$ and the same absolute tolerance. The new polynomial formula removes the logarithmic factor uniformly, gives explicit weights for the complex zeros, and preserves the two-field prime and first-failure conventions. Central cardinal splines, the Fourier transform of a power of sinc, and the relation between a counting exponent and absolute convergence are classical. Here the real-part Taylor correction, the bounded log support and the prime-side breakpoint estimate are used together. [First release](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-host-split-inert-race/releases/tag/fourth-root-spectrum-v1.0.0) · [Schoenberg](https://doi.org/10.1137/1.9781611970555) · [Büthe’s finite-zero prime bound](https://doi.org/10.1090/mcom/3060)

The eventual direct truncation follows from the classical Huxley–Jutila log-free density bound and the explicit Dirichlet zero-free region of Khale. The inverse criterion uses the magnitude of this specific dyadic transform on the right of the critical line. It characterizes its absolute tail. It gives no obstruction to cancellation in its signed tail.
