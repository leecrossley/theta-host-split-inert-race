# Prior work and scope of the density bound

Reviewed 21 November 2026. The claim concerns two entire signed windows of log widths $`6`$ and $`45/8`$, with $`b=r=2`$ on the 547/727 field. The starting heights are at a fixed log separation of $`15529862.885`$. I found no earlier publication with explicit uniform and finite-prefix lower logarithmic proportions for this synchronized event. The methods for proving recurrence and lower bounds for returns to a box are classical.

| Source | Relation to this result |
|---|---|
| Earlier 547/727 certificates | The previous paper finds the two one-way windows, an all-ordinate tail estimate and arbitrarily late one-way windows. Its compactness argument supplies no numerical density for two windows at a prescribed separation. The present proof reuses its signed finite head and its global absolute tail bound, and constructs a reproducible weighted phase partition. [Window certificate](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-race-factor-400/blob/f1773b07a55e76947d4e0c6ab19a112fd91fe6cf/PROOF.md) · [Original trace lemma](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-race-short-shifts/blob/483c31f2df2132ed2165620ea55e20e969228bfb/RaceTrace.lean#L83) |
| Rubinstein and Sarnak | They study logarithmic densities of pointwise rankings in prime races, including the role of hypotheses on zeros. Here an event requires two inequalities at every point of two real intervals. The quantitative bounds allow arbitrary dependencies between zero ordinates. [Chebyshev’s Bias](https://doi.org/10.1080/10586458.1994.10504289) |
| Akbary, Ng and Shahabi | Their limiting-distribution results cover arithmetic error terms, including Chebotarev errors, and vector-valued error terms. Existence of a limiting distribution alone gives neither of the two specific lower bounds here. [Limiting distributions of the classical error terms of prime number theory](https://www.cs.uleth.ca/~nathanng/RESEARCH/AkbaryNgShahabi.pdf) |
| Kaczorowski and Ramaré | Almost periodicity of normalized prime errors is an established route to recurrence. The application here uses individually certified coefficient budgets to give a numerical measure bound for a fixed pair of windows. [Almost periodicity of some error terms in prime number theory](https://ramare-olivier.github.io/Maths/kaczo.pdf) |
| Prime-ideal races | Hayani studies distributions and bias in prime-ideal races. The present event concerns signed changes of the explicitly weighted score on full intervals, with completion deadlines fixed in advance. [Chebyshev’s bias in prime ideal races](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23150) |
| Primitive Dirichlet theorem | The predecessor uses the independently verified primitive Dirichlet critical-line theorem. Its finite zero brackets and all-ordinate tail supply the arithmetic input here. [Theorem](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/371a1a26d5e17f604428e3a8ce6ce341cf517434/src/Main.lean#L12) · [Independent verification](https://math.mit.edu/~drew/grh/) |

Searches covered the field primes and radicand, the exact separation, translates of prime-race windows, simultaneous logarithmic density in prime-ideal races, vector-valued limiting distributions and almost periodicity of prime error terms. Public code was searched for the exact separation. The bounds use a Cauchy–Schwarz collision inequality and the elementary Fourier coefficients of a triangular function, both proved in the text. The numerical claim of optimality is confined to the stated dyadic allocation under the published rounded weights and budget. The search cannot establish absolute priority over unindexed or unpublished work.
