The earlier three-score paper identified the adjacent prime-norm knots of the unnormalised tent. The new finite result identifies the *optimal* factor at the original amplitude, determines the precise amplitude at which 18 fails through one billion, and gives the two branches of the function through amplitude 5. The touching minimum and both endpoint witnesses are evaluated from prime ideals. The recurrence part of the original paper retains its separate global zero hypothesis. ## Exact late exception intervals from one million to one billion

The preceding release settled the full range starting at 1 through one billion. Its worst small-amplitude wait came at height 1, and it left the earlier sufficient bound of 7 above one million. The present theorem identifies the exact separate late factors, gives every failing real height in $`10^6\le H\le10^9`$ for every $`C\ge6`$ and counts the failing integers at 6. The original 625-witness certificate is reused to discard all but three gaps. The six boundary crossings and the interpolation cover of their complete gaps are additional evidence.
