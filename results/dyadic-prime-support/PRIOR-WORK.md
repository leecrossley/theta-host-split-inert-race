# Prior work and scope

The question is exact numerical recovery of an individual $`\log_2 p`$ by a finite algebraic linear combination on the fixed dyadic grid of the 547/727 and 571/599 theta races. On 22 November 2026 I compared the maintained family index, the local Euler-factor, conductor-identification, Frobenius-window and consecutive-doubling papers, and searches combining the four conductors, prime races, dyadic sampling, exact linear recovery, prime logarithms and spline interpolation. I found no earlier determination of the three recoverable primes for these two fields, the minimum-support formula, the joint rank at every height, or the explicit all-direction bound used here. This search supports a priority claim for those specific statements. ## The minimum number of independently chosen grids

The predecessor edition allows an arbitrary algebraic phase and gives a complete table when exactly one phase is available. The earlier finite Euler-factor paper already obtains an odd-norm coefficient by a three-score second difference. Its identity gives the upper bound of three here. The lower bound asks whether *any* finite linear combination from two infinite grids, each chosen with the target known in advance, can recover that coefficient. The single-field table identifies every exception to the three-grid requirement. The pooled table adds exactly one prime beyond the two single-field lists.
