# Prior work and scope

Reviewed 21 November 2026. The claim concerns the two specified theta-host races at common starting heights, their joint level and doubling-move geometry at independent widths, and the numerical single-height comparisons. The published sources and code searched yielded no earlier statement of these bounds for this pair.

| Source | Relation to this result |
|---|---|
| Alpöge and the individual trace races | The original parameter recursion and the prior host releases give the fibre signs. The earlier 571/599 result controls one level and the 547/727 result controls one level or short increment. Their separate tail extrema could occur at unrelated heights. This proof checks the frequencies across both hosts to control joint projections. [Original recursion](https://github.com/Deicyde/riemann-hypothesis/blob/40e56bab026552e089d7afcfeff08d2defb9c005/src/Development/AbelianVarietyTheory/Proofs/DecoratedLatticeProofs/ThetaLiftChainHeckeParameterTwistNextTargetChar.lean#L47) · [547/727](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-race-short-shifts/blob/483c31f2df2132ed2165620ea55e20e969228bfb/PROOF.md) · [571/599](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-host-split-inert-race/blob/8fca7cad37185e3d7951f0dffff5e2442d1dfa24/PROOF.md) |
| Diamond and Ford–Konyagin | Positive trigonometric products and finite weak independence already turn the residues of a scalar transform into oscillation bounds. We apply the order-one test to every real projection of these four observables, including the phase change between a level and a move. [Diamond](https://personal.math.ubc.ca/~gerg/teaching/592-Fall2018/papers/1971.Diamond.pdf) · [Ford–Konyagin](https://personal.math.ubc.ca/~gerg/teaching/592-Fall2018/papers/2002.Ford_2.pdf) |
| Martin–Ng | Their work treats inclusivity and limiting distributions of ordinary residue-class races under hypotheses on zeros. Here the finite exclusions give convex-hull containment for a different statistic and single-height inequalities for its projections. The common-start average and a prescribed joint sign pattern at one late height are different assertions. [Inclusive prime number races](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.00088) |
| Platt | Finite-height Dirichlet zero verification covers the conductors in this computation. The selected zeros occur in known spectra. The new calculation excludes all mixed signed sums and opposite-host singleton tubes needed for these two races, with two independent evaluators. [Numerical computations concerning the GRH](https://arxiv.org/abs/1305.3087) |
| Fenchel and Bárány–Karasev | The theorem reducing the number of points needed for a connected subset of Euclidean space is classical. Four common starts here follow by applying that result after proving the numerical product of ellipses is in the ordinary convex hull. [Notes About the Carathéodory Number](https://www.renyi.hu/~barany/cikkek/131.pdf) |

The comparison included the maintained theta collection and public GitHub code. The first-publication assessment is limited to the material found. The six local Lean results verify projection and support inequalities. The interval calculations and the written analytic and geometric proof have their own verification records.
