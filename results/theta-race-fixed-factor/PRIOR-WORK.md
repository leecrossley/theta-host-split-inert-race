# Prior work and scope

Reviewed on 21 November 2026. The claim concerns one fixed field, score, threshold and multiplicative endpoint for **both** signed doubling moves. No earlier statement of the factor $`e^{175}`$ or proof of an explicit constant-factor deadline for this exact race emerged in the sources reviewed. The passage from an absolutely convergent zero series to qualitative recurrence is classical. The numerical value here comes from a fixed-width positive test and two global spectral-moment estimates.

| Source | Relation to the result |
|---|---|
| Trace and earlier waiting bound | The field, admissible theta datum, five low zeros and $`1.9735`$ threshold come from the 547/727 short-shift release. Its successor obtained $`e^{162000}H^{3001/2999}`$ with finite-height zero verification. Its support grew with $`\log H`$ to cover possible high off-line zeros. This proof uses a fixed triangular window, sums all distances from the tested frequencies by completed logarithmic derivatives, and obtains a uniform factor.
