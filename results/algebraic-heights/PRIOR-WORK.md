# Prior work and scope

Reviewed 21 November 2026. The question here is which exact algebraic linear identities can hold between actual finite prime-ideal scores at a common height. The second question concerns the arithmetic nature of the heights at which a sliding deadline changes status.

- Baker proved the independence over algebraic numbers of rationally independent logarithms of algebraic numbers. Unique factorization supplies the rational independence of the prime logarithms. This paper applies Baker’s theorem to the finite odd-prime expansions. [Baker, 1966](https://doi.org/10.1112/S0025579300003971) · [Baker, 1967](https://doi.org/10.1112/S0025579300003843)
- The prime number theorem in a fixed reduced progression supplies primes in every sufficiently late interval of a specified relative width. The arithmetic here selects two classes that isolate the two hosts and proves that their samples recover the complete nullspace. [Prime number theorem in progressions](https://kskedlaya.org/ant/chapter-5.html)
- Bordignon supplies the explicit progression estimates after a possible real zero is isolated. The present certificate covers all 43 real characters in the pair moduli and uses two moments to isolate every direction of the two local traces on each late interval. The searches also covered explicit Chebotarev and short progression bounds for the conductor pairs. [Published progression estimates](https://nyjm.albany.edu/j/2021/27-54v.pdf)
- Knot insertion and representations of a piecewise polynomial by jumps are classical. The geometric-grid polynomial division gives the exact coefficients for these normalized prime scores. The converse uses independence of prime logarithms and the two separating classes. [Boehm, 1980](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/0010448580901542)
