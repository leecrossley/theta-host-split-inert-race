## Counting exact inverse heights

The previous algebraic-height release proves transcendence at a fixed nonzero algebraic target and an individual quadratic degree bound. It leaves open how many independent square roots the different hits require. The 547/727 factor-18 result computes the first loss and several signed threshold intervals by two prime-sum methods. Here the knot sweep and direct tent-prefix logic build on those evaluations. The present calculation classifies all positive target magnitudes through one million on both hosts, and the two checkers reconstruct and separate the branch constants. [Original rank and crossing result](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-host-split-inert-race/releases/tag/algebraic-heights-v1.0.0) · [Earlier prime-cell computations](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-host-split-inert-race/blob/theta-race-factor-18-v1.2.0/results/theta-race-factor-18/prime_knots.py)

Independent square classes give independent choices of signs by classical Kummer theory. On these prime cells the classes have the form $`T^2-d`$. The contribution is to determine the numerical constants that occur, to bound their joint degree sharply on the finite range, and to prove that infinitely many are required on every tail for a residual set of actual amplitudes. The infinite argument uses Baker’s theorem, the prime number theorem in fixed progressions, recurrence of the signed scores, and the nonzero four-knot coefficient calculation. [Milne, Kummer theory](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/FT.pdf#page=75) · [Common-window theorem](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-host-split-inert-race/releases/tag/joint-levels-and-moves-v1.2.0)

## Ratios between actual crossing heights

Roy proved a lower bound on ranks of matrices of algebraic linear forms in logarithms. His strong six exponentials theorem bounds the dimension of the forms that remain logarithmic after multiplication by a fixed transcendental number. Waldschmidt gives both the product and matrix statements. Baker supplies the separate linear independence of prime logarithms. The new application controls algebraic quotients of actual starts and linear ranks of the two field readings on an entire geometric grid. [Roy](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0022314X9290081Y) · [Waldschmidt, section 3](https://webusers.imj-prg.fr/~michel.waldschmidt/articles/pdf/LegacyRamanujan2013Text.pdf#page=4) · [Baker](https://doi.org/10.1112/S0025579300003971)
