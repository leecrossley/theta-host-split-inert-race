# When a fourth prime-race crossing gives new elliptic points

Take the first crossings at magnitudes $`1/2,5/2,7/2`$ in units of $`\log2`$. Use gains on 547/727 or losses on 571/599. Their three heights generate a field $`K`$ and give points on $`E:V^2=U^3-U`$. **Adding any one further rational target in the first band leaves every point of $`E(K)`$ unchanged. Adding $`1/10`$ and $`7/10`$ together raises the rank modulo constant algebraic points from two to four.** These are statements about the fields generated by actual crossing heights on either host. [Proof of equality of the full groups](PROOF.md#a-fourth-measurement-and-the-full-group-of-points) · [Two-target rank and point counts](PROOF.md#two-rational-measurements-that-work-together)

| Targets on one host | Points on $`V^2=U^3-U`$ over the field of their first heights |
| --- | --- |
| Any four distinct positive targets with rational squares in the first band | Exact rank $`2h`$, where $`h`$ counts harmonic four-subsets of the five branch points. The possible ranks are 0, 2 and 4. |
| The fixed triple above and one new target $`t`$ with rational square | Rank four exactly when $`4t^2\in\{13,17,33,37,73,97\}`$. All six work on both fields. Every other such fourth target gives equality of the full groups. |
| A real algebraic fourth target without the rational-square restriction | Every open interval in the first band contains target values that add new points. |

The constants here form $`\overline{\mathbf Q}`$. The original triple has a generator over $`\mathbf Z[i]`$ modulo constants, recovered explicitly by halving the point of the preceding edition. The proof uses first-cell prime arithmetic, the character decomposition of a classical curve and a genus-two good-reduction check at 11. ## Algebraic curves from first prime-race crossings

The very first crossings on 547/727 and 571/599 give explicit points on the elliptic curve $`V^2=U^3-U`$. On either field, for each positive integer $`m`$, take the first hits at magnitudes $`1/(2m),5/(2m),7/(2m)`$ in units of $`\log2`$. Use gains on 547/727 and losses on 571/599. The resulting points on each host are linearly independent, even over the endomorphism ring modulo constant algebraic points. Their coordinates lie in the indicated multiquadratic extension of a function field. [Point coordinates and proof](PROOF.md#independent-points-on-one-elliptic-curve)

| First-hit result on either host | Exact conclusion |
| --- | --- |
| $`n`$ distinct positive real algebraic targets in its first band | The field of the heights has degree $`2^n`$ over $`\overline{\mathbf Q}(X)`$. Every product using all $`n`$ heights with nonzero integer exponents generates the full extension. |
| Three targets | The possible elliptic $`j`$-invariants are exactly the real algebraic numbers $`j\ge1728`$. The targets $`1,2,3`$ give $`470596/225`$. |
| A genus $`g`$ hyperelliptic curve with real algebraic branch points | $`2g+1`$ targets suffice to realize its quadratic function field over the displayed base. This number is minimal for that projection. |
| Heights from different fields | Across both complete first bands, at most one pair admits a nontrivial two-height power relation modulo algebraic numbers. |

The first cells involve only the prime 31 on 547/727 and norm $`7^2`$ on 571/599. They end at starting heights $`47/8`$ and $`79/8`$. At the named magnitude 1.9735, the quotient with the first hit at magnitude 2 is transcendental and has degree four over the host’s base. All these statements are unconditional. ## Exact hits and the rank threshold

The 547/727 race hits both exact thresholds $`+1.9735\log 2`$ and $`-1.9735\log 2`$ after every starting height $`H\ge1`$, within one fixed multiplicative window. **Every height where it hits either threshold is transcendental.** Through $`H=e^{12500}`$ both exact hits finish by $`400H`$. Every finite interior boundary of a missed $`400H`$ deadline is also transcendental. The arithmetic conclusions are unconditional. Locating the first misses numerically uses the separate zero conditions of the directional result, which GRH implies and Alpöge's announced Dirichlet GRH theorem supplies. [Crossings and deadlines](PROOF.md#exact-crossings-and-deadlines) · [Finite deadline](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-host-split-inert-race/releases/tag/theta-race-waiting-bound-v1.3.0) · [Directional first misses](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-host-split-inert-race/releases/tag/theta-race-factor-400-v1.3.0)

Take the width-two score at $`m`$ distinct positive algebraic multiples of one sufficiently large algebraic start on both the 547/727 and 571/599 fields. Divide each score by $`\log 2`$. The $`2m`$ numbers and 1 are linearly independent over the algebraic numbers. For mixed algebraic widths and dilations, at every sufficiently large algebraic start, a matrix gives **every algebraic linear relation** among the readings. Its columns contain the three changes of slope of a tent. [Rank theorem](PROOF.md#the-rank-at-algebraic-starts) · [Exact width refinement](PROOF.md#width-refinement-and-exceptional-real-starts)

For algebraic $`0\lt y\le e^{366}`$ the six readings at $`y,\sqrt2y,2y`$ have rank seven together with 1 exactly when $`y\gt79\sqrt2/8`$. Below and at the transition the first two readings on 571/599 use at most the logarithm of 7. Above it, exact determinants cover 901 cells and prime chains reach $`e^{366}`$. The fixed-modulus theorem gives full rank at all sufficiently large algebraic starts. Under Dirichlet GRH we can join the two ranges. [Rank transition](PROOF.md#the-exact-transition-at-the-three-scales) [Exact minors](evidence/initial-minors.tsv)

Assuming Dirichlet GRH, for every $`L\ge e^{366}`$ and each sign, the ordinary primes between $`L`$ and $`4L/3`$ whose two local splitting traces project above magnitude $`1/32`$ in that direction have total log weight greater than $`L/864`$. This holds for every unit projection and gives a quantitative count on the infinite range. [Prime bound and normalization](PROOF.md#an-explicit-infinite-range-and-a-prime-bound)

For either race, two rational starts in the same guaranteed window can bracket any nonzero real algebraic target of magnitude at most 5.85. The unique weight that gives this target by averaging the two normalised moves is transcendental. For a combination of the races with real algebraic coefficients $`v_A,v_B`$, the guaranteed range is $`0\lt |t|\le 5.85(|v_A|+|v_B|)`$. For the whole square of paired moves three rational starts suffice, and almost every target needs three. The corresponding sharp count for four coordinates is five. [Common windows and target range](PROOF.md#how-many-rational-starts-does-a-vector-target-need)

If an algebraic deadline fails but every rational height escapes a strict counterexample, the square of the logarithm of 2 must be a rational function of finitely many odd prime logarithms with algebraic coefficients. The proof gives its degree-two or degree-four equation. [Misses that a rational search could overlook](PROOF.md#what-an-isolated-missed-deadline-would-force)

Alpöge's announced Dirichlet GRH theorem supplies this input. It works directly with the prime-ideal scores. The rank is linear rank over the algebraic numbers.
