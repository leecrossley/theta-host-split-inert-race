# Prior work and scope

Reviewed 21 November 2026. The result concerns the precise logarithmic mean-square regularity of aligned, variable-width 547/727 prime races, including signed mixtures and the construction of smooth races at finite height. The proof uses the following established ingredients.

| Source | What it supplies |
|---|---|
| Ford, Soundararajan and Zaharescu | Section 3 and Lemma 2 give the zero count and the fixed-frequency Landau estimate for the Selberg class, including primitive Dirichlet functions. This makes different real log-width frequencies orthogonal in the normalized high-ordinate average. [Original paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0805.2745#page=13) |
| Wiener-type subsequence formulas | The diagonal mass of a measure and its squared Fourier coefficients are classically related. Cuny, Eisner and Farkas treat averages along subsequences and on the real line. Our numerator formula applies this diagonal argument to the eight zero multisets and feeds it into the regularity classification. [Cuny, Eisner and Farkas](https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.00101) |
| Individual Dirichlet pair correlation | Garunkštis and Paliulionytė prove a uniform formula on the required Fourier interval. Its positive triangular average controls the second moment of multiplicities. This yields the single logarithm in the squared displacement law. [Theorem 1.1](https://hrcak.srce.hr/en/file/495424) |
| Almost-periodic prime errors | Akbary, Ng and Shahabi establish limiting-distribution results for classical prime errors, including a Chebotarev error, using almost periodicity in mean square. The regularity index here concerns the function of log height, rather than smoothness of its value distribution. [Akbary, Ng and Shahabi](https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.1657) |
| Compact width kernels | Exponential splines have a general differential-operator theory. The present integral construction is the Green identity for the compact race kernel, applied exactly to the prime-only finite sum. [Unser and Blu](https://bigwww.epfl.ch/publications/unser0503.html) · [Width kernel and prime-only inversion](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-race-width-cusp/blob/52eee965886958193d2ff38595f92389b90f4b93/PROOF.md) |
| Earlier field-specific results | The width family and its oscillation, dyadic peak and sharp small-displacement maximum were previously available. The mean-square law, the atomic width obstruction and the smooth finite-height synthesis concern different observables. [Width family](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-race-short-shifts/blob/483c31f2df2132ed2165620ea55e20e969228bfb/PROOF.md) · [Peak theorem](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-race-isolated-peaks/blob/ee35ecfd698ab0ad193669a8109bbbfb1aac6049/PROOF.md) · [Cusp theorem](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-race-width-cusp/blob/52eee965886958193d2ff38595f92389b90f4b93/PROOF.md#the-cusp-at-every-width) |
| Global critical-line input | The primitive Dirichlet strip theorem puts the relevant ordinates on the line and supplies the prime-only limiting profile. [Pinned theorem](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/371a1a26d5e17f604428e3a8ce6ce341cf517434/src/Main.lean#L12) · [Sutherland's independent checks](https://math.mit.edu/~drew/grh/) |
