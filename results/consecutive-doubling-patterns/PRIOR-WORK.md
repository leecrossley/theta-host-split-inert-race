## First joint run and its dependence on the margin

Reviewed 22 November 2026. The maintained first edition already gave sixteen rational witnesses below 47,000 at margin 1.6, and all six-step sign words arbitrarily late under a sixteen-character zero-location condition. It did not determine the first height for the two-step catalogue, exclude all earlier real starts, or locate the different real and integer transitions. The present finite calculation counts prime ideals directly. The family’s earlier deadline paper treats the gain and loss of one host with freely placed dyadic moves. Here both hosts must gain on each of the same two consecutive doublings. The algebraicity obstruction at a positive algebraic margin specializes the family’s prime-logarithm result. [Original sign words](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-host-split-inert-race/releases/tag/consecutive-doubling-patterns-v1.0.1) · [Single-host deadlines](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-host-split-inert-race/releases/tag/theta-race-factor-18-v1.3.0) · [Algebraic heights](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-host-split-inert-race/blob/algebraic-heights-v1.7.1/results/algebraic-heights/PROOF.md)

Bays and Hudson computed crossing regions for cumulative prime counts modulo 4 and 8 by sieve. Rubinstein and Sarnak studied limiting distributions of residue-class races. Martin and Ng gave inclusivity results for such orderings. Fiorilli and Jouve proved unconditional biases in certain number-field races. These works concern different counts or different quantifiers. Baker’s linear independence theorem and the secant bounds for piecewise smooth functions are classical ingredients here. [Bays and Hudson](https://personal.math.ubc.ca/~gerg/teaching/592-Fall2018/papers/1979.Bays.pdf) · [Rubinstein and Sarnak](https://www.math.uwaterloo.ca/~mrubinst/publications/Chebyshev.pdf) · [Martin and Ng](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.00088) · [Fiorilli and Jouve](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.12245) · [Baker](https://doi.org/10.1112/S0025579300003971)

The search used both conductor pairs, first occurrences and crossing regions in Chebotarev races, runs on consecutive dyadic windows, finite versus integer starting heights, and the benchmarks 1.9735 and 20,306,534. Among the sources and public family editions examined, none gave these two prime-sum inverse branches on the whole margin interval, the two transition values, or the certified real and integer first crossings. The priority claim is scoped to this observable and these fields.

The phase-sampling counts use the elementary covering radius of points on a circle and the inverse function theorem at a transverse intersection. Equal spacing and the largest-gap argument are classical. The field-specific inputs are the unique certified early excursion, its endpoint at the integer threshold, its transverse slopes and the independently checked counts for both clocks. Searches on geometric sampling, phase offsets and first-passage thresholds uncovered no previous calculation for these two fields.
