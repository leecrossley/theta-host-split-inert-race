# Prior work and scope

Reviewed 21 November 2026. The result concerns six consecutive dyadic doublings on the two specified trace races, all 4,096 joint sequences at the stated margin and a common positive-density translation of their starting exponents. The search did not locate this catalogue, its certificates, or the numerical word bound for these fields.

| Source | What it covers |
|---|---|
| The two original theta-host races | The host calculations supply the local signs and Euler factors. The common-height theorem bounds every real projection and gives four finite examples for one doubling. A projection can reverse although a prescribed quadrant never occurs. The present result realizes every six-step pattern at arbitrarily late actual power-of-two starts. [547/727](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-race-short-shifts/blob/483c31f2df2132ed2165620ea55e20e969228bfb/PROOF.md) · [571/599](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-host-split-inert-race/blob/8fca7cad37185e3d7951f0dffff5e2442d1dfa24/PROOF.md) · [Common-height theorem](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-host-split-inert-race/blob/joint-levels-and-moves-v1.0.0/results/joint-levels-and-moves/PROOF.md) |
| Rubinstein–Sarnak and Martin–Ng | These papers study orderings and logarithmic densities in cumulative residue-class races. They supply the historical context for inclusivity. Our counts are compact prime-ideal windows observed at consecutive dyadic steps on two fixed fields. [Chebyshev's bias](https://www.math.uwaterloo.ca/~mrubinst/publications/Chebyshev.pdf) · [Inclusive prime number races](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.00088) |
| Leung | The joint short-interval theorem gives a limiting distribution under RH and linear independence for multiple ordinary-prime intervals in an asymptotic regime. Here two fixed hosts and fixed-ratio adjacent windows share one scale. Positive recurrence follows from the finite catalogue and compact phase returns. [Joint distribution of primes in multiple short intervals](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.04000) |
| One-host curvature and common returns | The compact-test gamma kernel, prime-power evaluation and almost periodic return argument were available. The earlier paired recurrence concerned two same-field intervals of fixed separation. Here the same translation preserves the full dyadic catalogue simultaneously. [Finite compact-test formula](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-race-curvature-euler-factors/blob/af7093b56a7f6b4155dba27e735b5b1d471ff707/PROOF.md) · [Paired recurrence](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-race-paired-density/blob/5181e3a8545c67f1bc93f344b39683cab4a10383/PROOF.md) |
| Bennett, Martin, O'Bryant and Rechnitzer | Their explicit zero-counting bound is used unchanged to bound the omitted spectral mass and the number of phase coordinates. The phase partition, Fourier tent minorant and pigeonhole arguments are standard. The numerical return, word and period bounds apply these methods to the two specified races. [Counting zeros of Dirichlet L-functions](https://doi.org/10.1090/mcom/3599) |

Searches covered the four conductors, successive dyadic prime races, prescribed sign patterns, comparative prime ideals, oscillations of shifted smoothed counts, compact-test explicit formulae and logarithmic density. Searches also covered sampling on exact geometric progressions, consecutive dyadic exponents, all four conductor labels together and finite signed Fourier certificates. A public GitHub code search for those labels together with dyadic patterns yielded no match. The priority assessment is limited to the public sources and code examined. The recurrence, word-count bound and monotone-run bounds all use Alpöge's announced primitive Dirichlet critical-line theorem (Dirichlet GRH), and so does the dyadic table through its bound for the unlisted zeros. The sixteen rational two-step witnesses use direct counts and can be checked independently of that theorem. [Verification record](https://math.mit.edu/~drew/grh/) · [Full proof](PROOF.md)
