# Prior work and scope

The question is the least log-height span of a positive normalized average that removes every nontrivial-zero term up to absolute ordinate one on two specified theta prime races. The bounds cover each field separately and one common average. The certificate also bounds the response left by every shorter filter.

| Source | Relation to the result |
|---|---|
| Kreĭn and Nudelʹman, and Bayer and Teichmann | Positive moment representation, separation by a signed test function and finite atomic quadrature are classical. The cosine moment curve used here depends on rigorously enclosed Dirichlet zeros. The upper certificates give specific rational nodes with strictly positive masses for the true ordinates. [The Markov Moment Problem and Extremal Problems](https://doi.org/10.1090/mmono/050) · [The proof of Tchakaloff's theorem](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0502473) |
| Kadiri and Platt | The positive real part of the completed logarithmic derivative outside the critical strip is a classical zero-counting tool. Platt verified critical-line location to heights well above one for these conductors. Here an 810-bracket certificate with two total-mass calculations counts all low zeros with multiplicity and proves the two bands disjoint. Platt's finite verification supplies context. [Explicit logarithmic derivatives](https://www.cs.uleth.ca/~kadiri/articles/zerosL-04-09-07.pdf) · [Numerical computations concerning the GRH](https://arxiv.org/abs/1305.3087) |
| Akbary, Ng and Shahabi, and de la Bretèche, Fiorilli and Jouve | Almost-periodic mean squares and smoothing arithmetic errors against test functions have extensive precedents. The present support-width question imposes nonnegative unit mass on the height shifts and requires exact transfer zeros simultaneously. [Limiting distributions of classical error terms](https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.1657) · [Moments in the Chebotarev density theorem](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.12899) |

The results here determine how wide a positive filter must be to remove the entire finite low band, construct such filters, and give a separate lower bound for width-20 averages against arbitrary comparison-field filters.
