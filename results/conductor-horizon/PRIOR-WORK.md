# Prior work and scope

Reviewed 21 November 2026. The object being identified is the unordered pair of prime conductors in the specified family. Every candidate omits its own ramified primes and the same fixed auxiliary set. This convention is part of the measurement. The target has the published 547/727 theta frame.

| Predecessor | Relation to this result |
|---|---|
| Classical splitting and arithmetic equivalence | The splitting of rational primes, residue degrees and their relation to zeta functions have an extensive literature. Perlis's theorem characterizes arithmetic equivalence via almost all splitting types. This note gives an exact observation schedule and a sharp height cutoff for one signed window and a restricted family. [On a question of Perlis and Stuart](https://nyjm.albany.edu/j/2019/25-25p.pdf) |
| Quadratic fields with prescribed local behavior | Varma counts quadratic fields with prescribed splitting at finitely many primes. For the prime-conductor replacements here, reciprocity and Dirichlet's theorem on progressions suffice. The absence of a contribution at a specified norm determines precisely how long the competitors can agree. [Varma, Section 4](https://www.math.utoronto.ca/~ila/raytorsionAccepted2021.pdf) · [Dirichlet's theorem](https://math.mit.edu/classes/18.785/2021fa/LectureNotes18.pdf) |
| Explicit primes in progressions | Theorem 1.2 of Bennett, Martin, O'Bryant and Rechnitzer yields a prime of a distinguishing residue class between a large height and twice that height. Their theorem gives the quantitative input for the pairwise tail statement. [Primary paper](https://personal.math.ubc.ca/~bennett/BeMaObRe-IJM-2018.pdf) |
| The 547/727 field and finite readout | The earlier work supplies the verified theta datum, character decomposition and exact finite inverse at a specified odd norm. Here the field is allowed to vary. The theorem determines when observations force both conductors and proves the matching obstruction by constructing infinitely many alternatives. [Field and trace](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-host-split-inert-race/blob/main/results/theta-race-short-shifts/PROOF.md) · [Finite inverse](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-host-split-inert-race/blob/main/results/theta-race-finite-euler-readout/PROOF.md) |
| Earlier finite sieve | The segmented residue-class sieve adapts the 547/727 factor-18 calculation. New fixed-point bounds retain enough precision to separate the two conductor alternatives at the larger norm. [Earlier sieve](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-host-split-inert-race/blob/main/results/theta-race-factor-18/sieve.cpp) |
