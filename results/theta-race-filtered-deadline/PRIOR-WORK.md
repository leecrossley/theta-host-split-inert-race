# Prior work and scope of the waiting bound

Reviewed 21 November 2026. For the 547/727 prime-ideal score with $`b=r=2`$, a factor of $`10^{18}`$ suffices for both moves above the normalized threshold 1.9735 after every real height $`H\ge1`$. I found no earlier all-height certificate with this upper factor for this race. The earlier field-specific factor was $`e^{175}`$.

| Source | Relation |
|---|---|
| Previous all-height upper factor | The earlier proof uses the same isolated conductor-547 zero and the same prime-only error formula. Its triangular averaging measure and signed tests give the factor $`e^{175}`$. The new symmetric positive filter isolates that frequency directly. Moving the filter centre through one carrier period supplies opposite signs. The remaining modes are bounded individually and two Poisson covers rule out missing low ordinates. [Earlier fixed-factor proof](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-host-split-inert-race/blob/main/results/theta-race-fixed-factor/PROOF.md) |

Previous one-way windows give a necessary lower factor and the present theorem gives a sufficient upper factor. [Trace](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-host-split-inert-race/blob/main/results/theta-race-short-shifts/RaceTrace.lean#L83) · [Finite height coverage](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-host-split-inert-race/blob/main/results/theta-race-factor-18/PROOF.md) · [One-way windows](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-host-split-inert-race/blob/main/results/theta-race-factor-400/PROOF.md) |
| Oscillation and frequency selection | Positive kernels, selecting zeros and forcing positive and negative excursions are established tools in analytic number theory. Kaczorowski and Ramaré study almost periodic errors, and Mossinghoff and Trudgian prove bounds on oscillations of weighted sums. Here the exact integer measure, completeness through ordinate 48, pointwise bridge and all-height factor form the field-specific certificate. [Kaczorowski and Ramaré](https://ramare-olivier.github.io/Maths/kaczo.pdf) · [Mossinghoff and Trudgian](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.14537) |
| Rigorous Dirichlet zero computations | Platt verified critical-line results to finite height for many primitive Dirichlet functions. The positive Poisson subtraction here identifies which ordinates can be missing from this particular signed table. Global control comes from GRH for the primitive Dirichlet functions, including the base field, and the earlier absolute tail estimates. [Platt](https://arxiv.org/abs/1305.3087) |

Searches covered the field primes and radicand, explicit waiting factors, the fixed threshold, both signs on multiplicative intervals, positive frequency filters for prime-race oscillations and public certificates for the 547/727 race. No claim of a new general Poisson formula or an optimal universal factor is made. Priority is assessed against indexed and accessible work.
