# Prior work and scope of the claim

Reviewed on 21 November 2026. The claim is the certified interval $`[1.008796429610,3.221800332553]`$ for the specified 547/727 profile, the two exterior sign tests that localize its component, and the resulting obstruction under Dirichlet GRH $`C=2\exp(2.213003902943)`$. I found no earlier publication of these results in the sources searched.

| Source | What the comparison establishes |
|---|---|
| Earlier 547/727 obstruction | The same race, threshold, limiting signal, recurrence argument and six small prime powers were already public. That proof bounded the profile on $`[1.014,3.214]`$, using an integral and a 256-term expansion with a uniform tail below 0.22. Its interval gives $`2\exp(11/5)`$. This paper sums the gamma term completely, independently integrates it at the sensitive left endpoint and encloses both ends of the component to one trillionth in log height. [Earlier proof](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-host-split-inert-race/blob/main/results/theta-race-factor-18/PROOF.md) · [Earlier series verifier](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-host-split-inert-race/blob/main/results/theta-race-factor-18/profile_series.py) |
| Earlier field and upper bound | The admissible fibre trace fixes the splitting sign. The universal upper factor $`e^{175}`$ comes from a positive test against an isolated zero. The present result concerns a lower bound on any admissible universal factor. [Field and trace](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-host-split-inert-race/blob/main/results/theta-race-short-shifts/RaceTrace.lean#L83) · [Fixed upper factor](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-host-split-inert-race/blob/main/results/theta-race-fixed-factor/PROOF.md) |
| Weil explicit formulas and compact tests | Conductor, prime-power and archimedean terms in explicit formulas are classical. Kadiri gives a Dirichlet explicit formula. Grenié and Molteni evaluate compact tests in a different class-group problem. The logarithm series, partial fractions and digamma identities used here are standard. The new numerical certificate uses their particular four translates for this score. [Kadiri](https://www.cs.uleth.ca/~kadiri/articles/explicit_zerofree_regions_for_dirichlet_l_functions.pdf) · [Grenié–Molteni](https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.02430) · [Digamma identities](https://dlmf.nist.gov/5.5) |

[Kaczorowski](https://matwbn.icm.edu.pl/ksiazki/aa/aa48/aa4844.pdf) · [Kaczorowski–Pintz](https://personal.math.ubc.ca/~gerg/teaching/592-Fall2018/papers/1987.Kaczorowski_2.pdf) · [Kaczorowski–Staś](https://bibliotekanauki.pl/articles/727867.pdf) · [Hayani](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23150) |
