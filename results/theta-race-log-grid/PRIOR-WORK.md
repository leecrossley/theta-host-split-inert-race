# Prior work and scope

Reviewed 21 November 2026. The claim is a uniform grid and a full log interval of width $`10^{-6}`$ for each sign of the dyadic 547/727 change, at every starting height $`H\ge1`$ and within the previously certified deadline. The offset of the grid may be prescribed arbitrarily. I found no earlier publication of that discretization or of these two field-specific certified moduli for the full profile.

| Source | Relation |
|---|---|
| Filtered all-height deadline | The preceding theorem supplies the positive averaging measure, margins, six intermediate heights, and the universal completion factor. Existence of two qualifying points alone supplies no explicit minimum width for their neighborhoods. The present proof obtains that width using a uniform modulus and separate finite-height estimates. [Filtered deadline](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-host-split-inert-race/blob/main/results/theta-race-filtered-deadline/PROOF.md) |

Here every height admits a qualifying interval for each direction, at the stronger prescribed magnitude. [Even moment](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-host-split-inert-race/blob/main/results/theta-race-distant-interval/PROOF.md) · [One-way windows](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-host-split-inert-race/blob/main/results/theta-race-factor-400/PROOF.md) |

The search used the field primes, radicand, geometric grids for prime-race changes, short-shift persistence, uniform moduli of prime-number error terms and the precise deadline and mesh. A public code search combined the field pair with “geometric grid”. The result gives a sufficient mesh. The coarsest grid width that works at every offset remains open. Unindexed or unpublished work could affect the priority assessment.
