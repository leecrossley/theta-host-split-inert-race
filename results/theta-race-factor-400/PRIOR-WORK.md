# Prior work and scope of the result

Reviewed 21 November 2026. The result concerns the dyadic 547/727 score on two explicit windows of log centres. One has only gains over width 6 and the other only losses over width $`45/8`$. Together they give explicit witnesses in both directions against $`400H`$. The six-unit window and its reflection give arbitrarily late obstructions to the factor $`2e^6`$. I found no earlier publication of these windows, these two explicit witnesses or a factor this large for this race.

## The four-digit obstruction

The original factor-400 paper supplied the explicit six-unit window, cubic moment majorant and almost-periodic translation argument. The trace identity comes from the earlier papers in the table above. The maintained extension proves a 6.235-unit interval at a different height. Both signs occur within it, and the loss stays below the required magnitude throughout. The field, score, phase convention and strict endpoint rule agree with the predecessor. The updated certificate uses 13,946 zero brackets, 9,946 of them new, and adds a degree-16 frequency expansion on an interval Fourier grid, a second whole-interval calculation and two interior sign witnesses. [Original factor-400 paper](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-race-factor-400/releases/tag/v1.0.0) · [Compact-window upper deadline](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-race-filtered-deadline/releases/tag/v1.0.0)
