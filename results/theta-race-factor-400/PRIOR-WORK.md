## The first failure

Earlier results gave occasional large-height misses and a rigorous positive initial segment through $`e^{12500}`$. They did not locate the first real starting height where the factor 400 fails. The new bracket compares the two bounding **heights** to within a factor of 1.0131, identifies the missed direction and pins the boundary doubling to the threshold. Its separate finite implication states, without using the global primitive theorem, the region where an odd-factor zero would have to lie to invalidate this localization.
