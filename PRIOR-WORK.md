# Priority and scope

Reviewed 21 November 2026. The claim is the pair of unconditional, width-independent numerical bounds for the compactly weighted split–inert prime-ideal race on the stated field, together with its trace realization. The four-ordinate certificate is part of the proof. An independent first-publication determination would require a wider review.
