# Prior work and scope

Reviewed 21 November 2026. I found no earlier formula that takes six specified values of the extremal second difference $`C(h)`$ of this 547/727 prime-ideal score and returns any specified local Euler factor. The relationship between L-function zeros and prime powers, exponential-spline filtering and recovery from power sums all have established precedents.

| Source | Relation |
|---|---|
| Guinand–Weil explicit formula and subsequent trace formulas | These connect the zero distribution with atomic prime-power data. Muñoz and Pérez-Marco give a general Poisson–Newton formulation. The new claim concerns a particular extremum measured from prime-score increments and a fixed finite sample count, including the exceptional calculation at the prime two. [Muñoz and Pérez-Marco](https://arxiv.org/abs/1309.1449) · [Burnol on Weil's local terms](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0101068) |
| Exponential splines | Unser and Blu develop exact discrete representations of differential operators on exponential splines. The present three-point calculation is an elementary instance of that principle. Our arithmetic uses the half-integer locations of the possible knots and the finite orders of the primitive characters. [Unser and Blu](https://bigwww.epfl.ch/publications/unser0503.html) |
| Compact prime-ideal kernel | The field-specific compact moment formula, character factorization and prime-power kernel were previously available. This proof reuses those formulas and credits their analytic ingredients. [Even compact formula](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-race-distant-interval/blob/527fffe7b7808457b47a292144fea96b556158a8/PROOF.md) · [Primitive character family](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-race-factor-400/blob/f1773b07a55e76947d4e0c6ab19a112fd91fe6cf/PROOF.md) · [Original smoothing kernel](https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.02430) |

[547/727 peak theorem](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-race-isolated-peaks/blob/ee35ecfd698ab0ad193669a8109bbbfb1aac6049/PROOF.md) · [Ordinary-prime extremal formula](https://github.com/leecrossley/prime-band-extrema/blob/d79c2c004855c423a240d82f8821232c1da849b6/README.md) |

The search combined the field primes, extremal symmetric second differences, inverse recovery of Euler factors, prime-power derivative jumps and exponential-spline inversion. A public code search combining the field primes with “curvature”, “Euler factor” and “prime race” returned no result. The local factors themselves are known. The claim concerns access to them through this extremal function, its exact sampling locations and the rounding budget. Unindexed or unpublished results could affect the priority assessment.
