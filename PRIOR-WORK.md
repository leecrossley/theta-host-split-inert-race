# Prior work and scope

Reviewed on 21 November 2026. The result under review is a negative answer to a specific numerical conjecture. The factor 18 covers every real starting height through one billion in this field's normalized dyadic prime race, yet for each sign separately even $2e^{11/5}$ fails at arbitrarily large starting heights. The searches and comparisons revealed no prior proof of this obstruction or of the interval certificate used to produce it. This is a scoped claim about the field and statistic.

The earlier waiting result certified $18H$ through one million and an all-height superlinear deadline. A finite success does not decide whether a fixed factor works globally.
