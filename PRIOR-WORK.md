# Prior work and scope

Reviewed 21 November 2026. I found no earlier result isolating all scores above 162 on this field throughout the real log-displacement band $`[1/2,10^9]`$, or proving the associated forty-unit drops and explicit upper and lower log densities. The exact maximum of a positive Fourier profile and the Hilbert-space triangle inequality are classical ingredients.

| Source | Relation |
|---|---|
| Earlier score theorem on this field | The weighted score, the admissible field and its theta signs come from this work. Its upper-limit result provides a lower bound on the peaks for a family of widths. The present result evaluates the exact upper endpoint at width two and bounds the heights surrounding every sufficiently high score. [Field construction and original score](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-race-short-shifts/blob/483c31f2df2132ed2165620ea55e20e969228bfb/PROOF.md) |
| Positive zero profiles for ordinary primes | The earlier ordinary-prime proof already aligns phases at one to attain an upper endpoint of a smoothed prime sum. Our use of simultaneous approximation follows that argument. The field-specific real-displacement isolation and density bounds require additional information about the zeros. [Ordinary-prime extremal formula](https://github.com/leecrossley/prime-band-extrema/blob/d79c2c004855c423a240d82f8821232c1da849b6/README.md) |
| Even moment and absolute mass | The earlier 547/727 window results evaluate the compact even moment, and the 6,000 signed brackets locate the positive ordinates used in both covers. The even-mass tail was independently checked by real gamma quadrature. [Even compact moment](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-race-distant-interval/blob/527fffe7b7808457b47a292144fea96b556158a8/PROOF.md) · [Bracket certificate](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-race-factor-400/blob/f1773b07a55e76947d4e0c6ab19a112fd91fe6cf/PROOF.md) · [Even-mass calculation](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-race-silent-powers/blob/2b119755f8d420d4cbeceab8dc2aa2c9fd0bbdcc/PROOF.md) |
| Exceptional dyadic powers | The finite-exponent result obtains opposite excursions separately at each integer power of two. Our result controls every real shift in a band simultaneously around the same centre. A single near-maximum score certifies both flanks. [Dyadic powers](https://github.com/leecrossley/theta-race-silent-powers/blob/2b119755f8d420d4cbeceab8dc2aa2c9fd0bbdcc/PROOF.md) |
| Arithmetic limiting distributions | Akbary, Ng and Shahabi establish general distribution results for arithmetic errors. Martin and Ng study inclusivity using conditions on zero relations. Neither comparison yields the explicit gap band and isolation threshold for this score. [Limiting distributions](https://www.cs.uleth.ca/~nathanng/RESEARCH/AkbaryNgShahabi.pdf) · [Inclusive prime number races](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.00088) |
| Critical-line theorem | The analytic input uses the independently verified primitive Dirichlet strip theorem. [Pinned statement](https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis/blob/371a1a26d5e17f604428e3a8ce6ce341cf517434/src/Main.lean#L12) · [Independent checks](https://math.mit.edu/~drew/grh/) |

The literature search combined the field primes, prime-ideal races, near-maximum scores, positive zero profiles, simultaneous dilation ranges and isolated peaks. The public code search for both primes, “isolated peaks” and “prime” returned twelve unrelated files. The optimal band and the true high-score densities are open. Unpublished or unindexed results could affect the priority assessment.
